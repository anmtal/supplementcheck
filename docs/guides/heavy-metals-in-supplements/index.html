Heavy Metals in Supplements: Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium & Mercury

Heavy metals are among the most common contaminants found in supplements — not added on purpose, but pulled from soil and water into the raw ingredients. Some product types carry far more than others.

Where heavy metals show up most

  • Protein powders — especially plant-based and chocolate. See the protein-powder guide →
  • Greens & superfood powders — concentrated plant material concentrates metals too.
  • Herbal & Ayurvedic products — some have been found with high lead, occasionally added in traditional preparations.
  • Rice-based products and kelp/seaweed — arsenic (rice) and metals/iodine (seaweed).

Why it matters

Lead, arsenic, cadmium and mercury are cumulative toxins with no safe daily dose. Children, pregnant people and daily users are most at risk.

How to lower your exposure

Favor third-party-tested products, vary your sources, don't exceed serving sizes, and consider an at-home heavy-metal test.

Frequently asked

Which supplements have the most heavy metals?
Plant-based protein powders, greens/superfood powders, some herbal and Ayurvedic products, rice-based products (arsenic) and seaweed. Concentrated plant material tends to concentrate metals.
Can I remove heavy metals from a supplement?
No. The practical steps are to choose third-party-tested products, vary your sources, and avoid exceeding serving sizes.
Are heavy metals in supplements regulated?
There is no single FDA limit for most supplements. California's Prop 65 sets warning thresholds, and third-party certifiers (USP, NSF) test for heavy metals, which is why certification is a useful signal.
