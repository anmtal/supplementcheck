Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-11. SupplementCheck is a free, informational site, and we keep data collection to a minimum.

What we collect

Browsing requires no account and no personal information. The only personal data we collect is your email address — and only if you choose to subscribe to recall alerts.

Email alerts

If you subscribe, your address is stored and emailed by our third-party email provider on our behalf, solely to send you new-recall alerts. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. We never sell or share your email.

Cookies & advertising

The site sets no advertising cookies today. If we add analytics or ads in future, we will update this page and request consent where required.

Data sources

Recall information is public-record data from the U.S. FDA via the openFDA API. We are an independent aggregator and are not affiliated with the FDA or NIH.
