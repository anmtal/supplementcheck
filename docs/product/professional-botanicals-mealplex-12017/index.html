Professional Botanicals MealPlex

Professional Botanicals · Herbal & botanical
!
🟡 RECALLED — UNDECLARED ALLERGEN

Professional Botanicals announces a voluntary field action for MealPlex and Pearl due to undeclared milk allergen.

SOURCE: openFDA / FDA enforcement · recall F-0351-2017 · Oct 5, 2016 · view record ↗
Recall classification
Class I
Class I — the most serious: reasonable probability of serious harm or death.
Issue type
Undeclared allergen
Recall status: Terminated.
Recalling firm
Professional Botanicals
Origin: Ogden, UT.
Where it was distributed
Nationwide. No VA/govt/military, Canadian, or other foreign consignees.
Recall initiated Oct 5, 2016.

What this means & what to do

If you have the allergy named in the recall, do not consume this product — an undeclared allergen can trigger a serious reaction. If you don’t have that allergy, the specific risk from this recall is low.
